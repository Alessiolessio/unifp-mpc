# UniFP-MPC recording gallery

Each recording below illustrates a named condition. Counts and uncertainty estimates come from the complete cohorts in the [research report](research_report.md). A denotes ordinary commands, B conditioned commands and C reachable matched ordinary commands. A pair of integers denotes physical scene / CEM seed. Fixed-command cases have a physical seed and no optimizer. All displayed times are simulated seconds after warmup unless the original clip labels its warmup separately.

## Reading and reproducing the recordings

A dashboard's future curves are the winning candidate at the indicated replan. Its history contains actual executed samples. Virtual commands, projected targets and integrated physical loads have different meanings. Pose-only cameras and cursor films above use the channels that were retained. The fixed tether, payload and wall films are measured time histories. Their cursor advances at physical time. Physical fixed-command films show the archived joint poses against the reconstructed static fixture. No MPC forecasts exist in those experiments. [Telemetry definitions](episode_telemetry.md) · [Reproduction guide](research_evidence.md)

MP4s in this report's media directory can travel with the HTML. Links under the archive require the original local artifact tree. The [video manifest](report_data/verified_media.csv) records codec dimensions, playback duration and SHA-256 for the selected files.
